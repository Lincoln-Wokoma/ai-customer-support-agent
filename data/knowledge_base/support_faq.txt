CUSTOMER SUPPORT FAQ

About Our Store
We are an online electronics store selling smartphones, laptops, tablets, accessories, and other consumer electronics.

Delivery
Orders within major cities are usually delivered within 2 to 4 business days.
Orders outside major cities are usually delivered within 4 to 7 business days.
Customers receive an order tracking number after their order has been dispatched.

Order Tracking
Customers can track their orders using the tracking number sent to their email or phone number after dispatch.
If a tracking number has not been received within 24 hours of dispatch, the customer should contact customer support.

Returns
Customers can request a return within 7 days of receiving an item.
The item must be unused and in its original packaging.
Some products may not be eligible for return because of hygiene or safety restrictions.

Damaged Products
If a customer receives a damaged product, they should contact customer support within 48 hours of delivery.
The customer may be required to provide photographs or videos showing the damage.
After verification, the store may offer a replacement, repair, or refund depending on the situation.

Refunds
Approved refunds are normally processed within 5 to 10 business days.
The exact time for the refund to appear depends on the customer's payment provider.

Payment Methods
Customers can pay using debit cards, bank transfers, and other payment methods available during checkout.

Order Cancellation
Customers may request cancellation before an order has been dispatched.
Once an order has been dispatched, cancellation may no longer be possible and the customer may need to use the return process.

Customer Support
Customers can contact support for questions about orders, payments, delivery, returns, refunds, and damaged products.
